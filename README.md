# 📊 Sales Dashboard 2026 — Excel

An interactive sales dashboard built in **Microsoft Excel** for an outdoor & sports equipment company operating across Europe. It turns raw transaction data into clear insights on revenue, profit, products, and regions, all filterable with one click.

![Dashboard Preview](dashboard.png)

---

## 🎯 Project Goal

Management needed a single view to answer key business questions:

- How are sales and profit trending month by month?
- Which product categories and products drive the most revenue?
- Which European countries and regions perform best?
- How do different customer types and sales channels compare?

---

## 🧩 Dashboard Features

| Section | Description |
|---|---|
| **KPI Cards** | Total Sales, Gross Profit, Average Price, Units Sold |
| **Revenue & Gross Profit** | Monthly trend line chart (Jan – Dec 2026) |
| **Revenue by Product Category** | Bar chart: Camping, Hiking, Travel, Cycling, Fitness |
| **Top 5 Bestsellers** | Best-performing products by units sold |
| **Sales Map** | Filled map of revenue by European country |
| **Interactive Filters** | Timeline (Order Date) and slicers for Region, Customer Type, Sales Channel |

All charts update instantly when a filter is applied.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- **Excel PivotTables & PivotCharts** for data aggregation
- **Slicers & Timeline** connected to all pivots for interactivity
- **Filled Map Chart** for geographic analysis
- **Custom number formatting** (`#,##0.0,"K"`) for clean, readable values
- **Data cleaning & structuring** in Excel Tables
- **Dashboard design**: consistent color palette, card layout, visual hierarchy

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- 📈 **July** was the peak month with **25.5K** revenue and **12.2K** gross profit; **February** was the weakest.
- 🏕️ **Camping (63.9K)** and **Hiking (61.8K)** are the top categories, together making up more than half of category revenue.
- 🎒 **Summit Backpack 45L** is the #1 bestseller (164 units), followed by **Compact Sleeping Bag** (146).
- 🌍 **Germany** and the **United Kingdom** lead in revenue across Europe.
- 📉 Sales slow down in Q4 after the summer peak, a pattern worth planning promotions around.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download `Sales_Dashboard_2026.xlsx`
2. Open it in **Excel 365 / Excel 2019+** (required for the Map chart)
3. Go to the **Dashboard** sheet
4. Use the slicers and timeline to explore the data

> The Map chart needs an internet connection to load.
